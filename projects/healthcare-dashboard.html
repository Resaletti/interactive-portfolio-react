Healthcare Operations & Cost Intelligence Dashboard

Executive dashboard designed to analyze patient admissions, operational efficiency, insurance behavior and healthcare costs.

Business Problem

Healthcare organizations often manage thousands of patient records, admissions, insurance providers and operational costs. Without centralized analytics, identifying trends and making informed decisions becomes difficult.

Solution

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard integrating patient admissions, insurance data, operational indicators and financial metrics into a single executive view, enabling fast and data-driven decision making.

Business Impact

  • Improved operational visibility.
  • Centralized KPI monitoring.
  • Faster executive reporting.
  • Better resource allocation support.

Technology Stack

Power BI DAX Power Query SQL Excel

Key Features

Interactive KPIs

Executive indicators providing a real-time overview of admissions, operational costs and healthcare performance.

Dynamic Filtering

Interactive filters allowing users to analyze data by department, insurance provider and time period.

Operational Insights

Visual reports designed to identify bottlenecks, trends and opportunities for operational improvement.

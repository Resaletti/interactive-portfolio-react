Stock Market Analysis & Investment Dashboard

Interactive dashboard designed to analyze stock market trends, portfolio performance, financial indicators and investment opportunities through dynamic visualizations.

Business Problem

Financial markets generate massive volumes of data every day. Without analytical dashboards, investors and analysts struggle to identify trends, compare assets and monitor portfolio performance efficiently.

Solution

Developed an interactive analytics dashboard providing market trend analysis, portfolio monitoring and financial performance visualizations to support data-driven investment decisions.

Business Impact

  • Market trend analysis.
  • Portfolio performance monitoring.
  • Financial KPI visualization.
  • Investment decision support.

Technology Stack

Power BI DAX Financial Analytics Excel Data Visualization

Key Features

Interactive KPIs

Executive indicators providing a real-time overview of admissions, operational costs and healthcare performance.

Dynamic Filtering

Interactive filters allowing users to analyze data by department, insurance provider and time period.

Operational Insights

Visual reports designed to identify bottlenecks, trends and opportunities for operational improvement.

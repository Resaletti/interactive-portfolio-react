Logistics Delivery & Operations Dashboard

Executive dashboard developed to monitor delivery performance, shipping efficiency, transportation costs and operational KPIs through interactive business intelligence visualizations.

Business Problem

Logistics companies generate large volumes of operational data, making it difficult to identify delivery delays, transportation costs and performance bottlenecks without centralized analytics.

Solution

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard integrating delivery performance, shipping operations, transportation costs and KPI monitoring into a single executive reporting solution.

Business Impact

  • Delivery performance monitoring.
  • Transportation cost analysis.
  • Operational bottleneck identification.
  • Executive logistics reporting.

Technology Stack

Power BI DAX Power Query SQL Excel

Key Features

Interactive KPIs

Executive indicators providing a real-time overview of admissions, operational costs and healthcare performance.

Dynamic Filtering

Interactive filters allowing users to analyze data by department, insurance provider and time period.

Operational Insights

Visual reports designed to identify bottlenecks, trends and opportunities for operational improvement.

Sales Intelligence & Business Performance Dashboard

Executive dashboard designed to analyze revenue, profitability, sales performance, customer behavior and business KPIs through interactive visualizations.

Business Problem

Sales organizations generate large volumes of transactional data, making it difficult to identify revenue trends, profitability, customer behavior and opportunities for business growth without centralized analytics.

Solution

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard integrating sales performance, profitability, customer segmentation and KPI monitoring into a single executive reporting solution.

Business Impact

  • Revenue performance monitoring.
  • Profitability analysis.
  • Customer segmentation insights.
  • Executive KPI reporting.

Technology Stack

Power BI DAX Power Query SQL Excel

Key Features

Interactive KPIs

Executive indicators providing a real-time overview of admissions, operational costs and healthcare performance.

Dynamic Filtering

Interactive filters allowing users to analyze data by department, insurance provider and time period.

Operational Insights

Visual reports designed to identify bottlenecks, trends and opportunities for operational improvement.
